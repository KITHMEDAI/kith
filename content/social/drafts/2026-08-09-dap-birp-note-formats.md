## Twitter/X

Kith generates DAP and BIRP notes now, not just SOAP and EMDR. BIRP in particular comes up a lot for insurance/agency documentation - "Intervention" is its own field, separate from what the client did and how they responded, not just SOAP relabeled.

## Instagram

Documentation formats aren't interchangeable the way they can look from the outside. SOAP works well for a lot of practices, but it doesn't map cleanly onto every requirement - agency and insurance documentation often specifically asks for BIRP, and high-volume practices sometimes want the simpler three-field structure of DAP instead.

Kith now generates both, alongside SOAP and EMDR. BIRP's "Intervention" field is real added structure, not a relabel - it documents what the clinician actually did in session, kept separate from what the client reported (Behavior) and how they responded (Response).

Pick the format at signup or switch it later in Settings. The note pipeline handles the rest.
