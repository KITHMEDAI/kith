### dictated-shift-handover ✓
- Objectivity – 'pretty chuffed' is reported without quotation marks as if it were the note's own neutral description; it should appear as Darren said he was 'pretty chuffed' or be paraphrased as 'Darren expressed satisfaction with his progress' – though the note does attribute it to Jess, the phrasing is colloquial and not in quotes.

- Required fields – Darren's last name is absent and not flagged as missing; NDIS documentation requires full participant name for audit and billing purposes.
- Required fields – Jess's last name / staff ID is absent and not flagged; required for billing evidence and worker accountability.
- Required fields – The incident section says 'None reported by the worker: confirm before signing', which is appropriate hedging, but the note should simply state 'No incidents reported' or explicitly instruct the worker to confirm, not leave it ambiguous for an auditor reading the signed copy.
- Audit-ready – No NDIS support item / line-item reference or support category is recorded; without this, the note cannot independently serve as billing evidence.
- Audit-ready – Duration of water session ('approximately twenty minutes') is useful but the total community-access support hours are not explicitly confirmed against the 4-hour shift, leaving a gap for an auditor verifying billable hours.
- Objectivity – 'a bit frustrated' is Jess's characterisation, not a direct observable description; the note should record the observable behaviour (e.g., 'Darren raised his voice / paced at the bus stop') and note that he self-regulated using breathing exercises, rather than relying solely on the worker's interpretive label.
