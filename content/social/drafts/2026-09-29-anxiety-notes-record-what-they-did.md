## Twitter/X

Anxiety notes usually record what the client felt, not what they did.

"Racing heart before the presentation" is half the story. "Racing heart, urge to leave, stayed and presented" is the other half.

Approach vs. avoidance is often the clearest progress marker. Write it down.
