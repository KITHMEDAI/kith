## Twitter/X

A recurring online session in Kith gets a fresh Google Meet link generated for every occurrence, not the same link copied three weeks ago and quietly gone stale. One less way a session starts fifteen minutes late hunting for the right invite.

## Instagram

There's a specific kind of session delay that has nothing to do with the client running late: the therapist can't find the right meeting link. It got buried in an old calendar invite, or got copied wrong three sessions ago, and now the first few minutes of the session are gone to logistics instead of the work.

Recurring appointments don't actually need one reused link - they need a correct one, every time. In Kith, each occurrence of a recurring session generates its own Google Meet link automatically, tied to that specific appointment.

It's not a feature anyone puts in a pitch deck. It's just one less way an online session starts late for reasons that have nothing to do with the client or the work.
