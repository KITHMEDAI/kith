## Instagram

The paperwork tax on therapy isn't really about how fast you type.

It's that by the time you sit down to write notes, the session's over — and you're reconstructing what happened from memory, which is a slower, less accurate task than writing something down as it's actually said.

Ambient transcription closes that gap. The session gets transcribed as it happens — in person or over a video call — and the note gets built from a real transcript instead of a memory of one.

We wrote up how it actually works (and what it doesn't replace) on the blog — link in bio.
