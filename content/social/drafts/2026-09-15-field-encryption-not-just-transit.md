## Twitter/X

Most "we encrypt your data" claims mean the connection is encrypted, not the data itself once it's stored. Kith field-encrypts sensitive data at rest under DPDP 2023 - patient notes, even the OAuth tokens behind calendar sync - not just the pipe it travels through.
