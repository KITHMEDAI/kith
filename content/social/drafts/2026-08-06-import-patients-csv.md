## Twitter/X

Switching practice management tools usually means retyping every patient by hand until you give up and just keep the old spreadsheet open in another tab. Kith imports patients straight from a CSV or Excel export - no manual re-entry to get started.

## Instagram

The real cost of switching practice management software usually isn't the software - it's the migration. Retyping every existing patient's name, contact details, and history by hand is enough of a tax that a lot of therapists just... don't switch, and keep juggling a spreadsheet alongside whatever tool they're using.

Kith imports patients directly from a CSV or Excel export, so your existing caseload is there on day one instead of trickling in over weeks of manual entry.

Not a flashy feature. Just the difference between actually starting and finding a reason to put it off.
