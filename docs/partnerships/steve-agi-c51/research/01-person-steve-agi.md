# Due diligence: Steve Agi (C51 Consulting / AGI360 / KAOS AI)

Research date: 2026-10-18. Public sources only: web search, public websites, the ABN Lookup register, Wayback Machine, RDAP domain records, and the public JavaScript bundles of his own sites. Nothing was logged into, submitted or contacted.

Labels: **CONFIRMED** means a primary or near-primary source is linked. **LIKELY** means strong circumstantial or third-party evidence. **UNVERIFIED** means it is claimed but could not be checked.

---

## Summary verdict

Steve Agi is a **real, identifiable Melbourne businessman with a long public footprint**. He co-owned the well-known burger brand Cafe 51 / #burgerlove (1994 to early 2020). He published a Greek-community magazine (OPA!, 2010 to 2013). Since 2021 he has run a small consultancy, C51 Consulting, through an active Australian company (KAPAGI CONSULTING PTY LTD, ABN 59 647 485 783, GST-registered). **I found no evidence of scams, fraud, lawsuits, insolvencies, director bans or complaints** linked to him or his entities. Searches here cannot prove there are none, and paid ASIC extracts were not pulled.

He is **not a healthcare, NDIS or health-tech operator**. He has no clinical, provider or NDIS track record I could find. His background is hospitality, marketing and brand work, followed by a run of trend pivots: ghost kitchens, then NFTs/crypto/VR, then a Square POS resale offer, then "AGI"/AI agents, now AI ops/compliance and a Bitcoin-mining farm-heat venture.

His current businesses are mostly 2026-vintage, solo-built AI no-code sites (Bolt.new, Vercel, WordPress). They have **no named clients, testimonials or delivered case studies**. AGI360's own business model is **charging founders fees up front** (A$1,950 assessment; A$950 onboarding + A$990/month/industry). He also **already runs his own "NDIS Documentation Assist" demo**, which overlaps directly with what he is proposing Kith build.

Bottom line: this is **probably not a scam, but it is a high execution and IP risk**. He is a credible networker and promoter with no demonstrated health-sector distribution. The main danger is not theft of money. It is Kith doing unpaid build work that ends up inside his KAOS/AGI360 product family, with "shared upside" that never turns into revenue. **Proceed only with a written agreement, a named contracting entity, IP retained by Kith, and paid or milestone-funded work.**

## Healthcare/NDIS credibility

- **No healthcare, NDIS, aged-care, disability or health-tech operating history found.** None of his career entries, publications or press touch healthcare. (CONFIRMED absence in public sources.)
- Healthcare and NDIS appear only as **target sectors** on the Aug 2026 relaunch of C51 ("Aged care & healthcare", "NDIS & community services") and as KAOS demo modules.
- AGI360's "case examples" include an anonymous entry: sector "Health & NDIS", problem "Health sector admin overhead", evidence "Commercial model defined, pilot site identified", next step "Paid pilot with health provider", status "Validation". **It names no provider.** It could describe the Kith proposal itself, or a placeholder. Ask him directly.
- He **already has his own NDIS Documentation Assist prototype** (AI-generated progress-note drafts with missing-info flags, OpenAI-backed). That is a potential competitor, a reason he wants a real product partner, and an IP-contamination risk.
- His demo's privacy notes say data goes to OpenAI and that "retention and deletion scope must be confirmed before live-data deployment". That suggests he knows about compliance in principle but has not deployed with real participant data.
- No evidence of NDIS provider registration, NDIS Commission links, AHPRA-registered partners, health-industry associations, or relationships with plan managers or providers.

## Red flags

1. **His business model is founder-pays.** AGI360 charges founders A$1,950 for an assessment and A$950 + A$990/month per industry (minimum 2) for "market access", with heavy "no guarantee of outcomes" language. A "shared-upside partnership" can later turn into a request for fees or for free build work. (CONFIRMED pricing)
2. **He has a directly overlapping product.** His KAOS "NDIS Documentation Assist" demo means anything Kith builds or shows could be folded into his product family. IP and non-compete terms are essential. (CONFIRMED)
3. **Serial trend pivots and no visible delivery record since 2020.** Ghost kitchens, then NFT/crypto/VR, then POS resale, then "AGI", then AI ops, then Bitcoin farm heat. Many sub-brands (C51, KAOS, CONTROL OS, AI is OK, AGI360, Crypto Crops, SLAM, Business Pulse). I found no named client, completed pilot, revenue figure or third-party case study for any post-2020 venture. (CONFIRMED pattern; outcomes UNVERIFIED)
4. **New, thin infrastructure.** AGI360 is about 7 weeks old, built on Bolt.new, with a Gmail contact address. KAOS demos run on bolt.host/vercel.app subdomains. This is typical of a solo operator, not an established firm. (CONFIRMED)
5. **Contracting-entity ambiguity.** The trading company KAPAGI appears to have someone else (likely Kalliopi Anagnostou) as director. Any agreement must name the legal entity and an authorised signatory. (LIKELY)
6. **Inconsistent or unverifiable claims.** "Founded in 2011" vs a 2021 company and a 1994 Cafe 51 lineage. The 2021 site said C51 was one of the first to establish virtual brands in Australia "over 5 years ago", with no evidence. The Westpac award and the City of Melbourne/Visit Victoria AR project could not be verified. (UNVERIFIED)
7. **Crypto adjacency.** NFTs (2021-23), an OpenSea portfolio, "AI + Blockchain Strategist", stablecoin posts, and the Crypto Crops investor pitch. This is not illegal, but it is a credibility and reputational consideration for a health-data product. (CONFIRMED)

**Not found (absence of evidence):** scam reports, Fair Work cases, VCAT/court matters, ASIC insolvency notices or director bans, or negative reviews tied to Steve Agi, C51, KAPAGI or Burgerlove. Search hits about a Melbourne "burger bar" penalty (Rum Runner / Burger Buzz) concern a **different** operator.

## Green flags

- Real, long-standing public identity in Melbourne, consistent across 2011 to 2026 sources (Uber, SmartCompany, Macquarie Uni record, podcast, LinkedIn).
- Built and ran a recognisable consumer brand (Cafe 51 / #burgerlove, up to five stores) for about 25 years. That shows real operating and marketing ability.
- Active, GST-registered Australian company since Jan 2021. Clear contact details: phone +61 478 834 818, a branded email, an ABN on the site.
- His sites carry careful, lawyer-style disclaimers (AFSL/CSF partners for regulated offers, labelled sponsored content, no guaranteed outcomes). That suggests he knows about compliance rather than intends deception.
- Large LinkedIn audience (about 14K followers), which is useful for distribution and awareness in Australia.
- He understands the NDIS documentation pain point well enough to have prototyped it.

## Open questions to ask him directly

1. Which legal entity would sign with Kith, and who is its director? Is it KAPAGI CONSULTING PTY LTD (ACN 647 485 783)? What is your role in it?
2. Name **one or two current paying clients** or completed pilots for C51/KAOS/AGI360 since 2021 that we can speak to.
3. Who is the "pilot site identified" / "paid pilot with health provider" in AGI360's Health & NDIS example? Is there a signed LOI? Is it a registered NDIS provider?
4. What is your relationship to the **NDIS Documentation Assist** demo? If we partner, what happens to it? Will you commit in writing not to build or sell a competing product?
5. In "shared upside", **who pays for development**? Is there any fee, retainer, or AGI360 assessment / market-access charge expected from Kith now or later?
6. Who owns the IP, code, data, brand and customer contracts? Who holds the provider relationships? What are exit and termination terms?
7. Who is the data controller under the Australian Privacy Act / APPs? Who handles NDIS Practice Standards obligations, data residency (AU hosting) and incident response?
8. Which healthcare or NDIS operators, plan managers or allied-health clinicians are already committed? Can you introduce one before any build starts?
9. What happened with the ghost-kitchen franchise rollout (2021) and Crypto Crops? Who were the counterparties and outcomes?
10. Can you substantiate the Westpac Top 20 (2018) and City of Melbourne / Visit Victoria AR project?
