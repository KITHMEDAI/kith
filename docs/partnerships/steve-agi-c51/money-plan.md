## Why "Kith invoices" is the key protection

The real risk isn't losing money (we pay nothing). It's building and delivering, the
customer paying C51, and the money never reaching Kith. Direct invoicing means:
- we can't be left unpaid for delivered work
- Kith holds the customer contract and relationship (exposure + connections)
- C51's share comes from real revenue, so its incentive is to bring paying customers

**Fallback if C51 insists on invoicing:** C51 pays Kith within 14 days of receiving
payment; Kith can see customer invoices; Kith is named in the customer agreement.
