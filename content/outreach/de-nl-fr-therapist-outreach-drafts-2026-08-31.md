## 1. Elmar Stamm (Praxis für Psychotherapie Elmar Stamm, Berlin, solo + 1 associate)

**To:** mail@praxis-stamm.eu
**Subject:** A free note-writing tool for behavioral/schema practice

Hi Elmar,

My name is Anurag, and I'm building Kith — a free clinical documentation tool for therapists, built around GDPR from the ground up rather than a US HIPAA product adapted after the fact.

After 25+ years in behavioral and schema therapy, I'd guess documentation is more of a "get it done" chore than a learning curve at this point — Kith listens through your device mic (in person) or a notetaker bot on Google Meet (online) and drafts a structured note afterward, so you're editing rather than writing from a blank page. Recording and AI-processing consent are two separate opt-ins, not one bundled checkbox.

It's free, no card required. If you'd ever want to try it on a real session and tell me honestly what's wrong with it, I'd welcome that.

Best,
Anurag Chaudhary
