# Kith Workspace: pricing (draft to discuss with Steve)

Full working (costs, competitors, margins): `docs/product/workspace-pricing-proposal.md`. Real-data test behind these limits: `docs/product/real-data-findings.md`.
All prices are AUD incl. GST. Nothing is final.

## Structure

**14-day free trial**
- Everything switched on, no card needed.
- 5 hours of recording included.
- After 14 days the account becomes read-only: notes can still be opened, searched, exported and deleted, but no new notes can be made.

**Essentials: A$19/month** (A$15/month billed yearly)
- Unlimited typed / pasted notes.
- In-person recording (phone or laptop mic), up to **60 min per recording**, 8 hours a month.
- Ask your notes (200 questions a month), AI edits, PDF export, data stored in Sydney.

**Pro: A$45/month** (A$36/month billed yearly)
- Everything in Essentials.
- **Online meetings** (Google Meet, Teams, Zoom: no bot joins the call).
- Recordings up to **2 hours** (3 hours coming), 25 hours a month.
- Unlimited Ask.

**Team (for providers): A$35 per user/month** (A$29 billed yearly)
- Pro features, minimum 5 users, invoiced.
- 30-day team trial.

For reference:
- NDIS note apps charge about A$15–19/month.
- Meeting note-takers (Otter, Fireflies) charge about A$26–46/month.
- Clinical scribes (Heidi, Lyrebird) charge A$150+/month.

## Questions for Steve

1. **Price point.** Is A$19 right for Essentials, or do NDIS support workers and sole traders expect A$15 or less?
2. **Who pays.** In your network, does the worker pay for their own tool, or does the provider buy it for the team?
3. **Team plan.** Does A$35 per user/month with a minimum of 5 users feel right for a provider? What do they pay for tools like ShiftCare today?
4. **Recording length.** Is 60 minutes enough for a shift / visit note, and is 2 hours (3 hours later) enough for the longest meetings your people sit in?
5. **Online meetings.** Should meetings be part of Pro, or a separate add-on?
6. **Trial.** Is 14 days and 5 hours of recording enough to decide? Should providers get 30 days?
7. **Card up front.** Would asking for a card to start the trial put your contacts off?
8. **Billing.** Monthly, yearly, or both? Card only, or do providers need invoices / bank transfer?
9. **Must-haves.** Is there anything that would stop a provider paying: an NDIS audit format, a specific note template, exporting to their system (ShiftCare, etc.)?
10. **First customers.** Which 2–3 providers or people would you put on a trial first, and what would they need to see to pay?
11. **Real test recordings.** Could 2–3 of your people record a short, consented sample (a shift summary, a home visit, a team meeting) so we can test with Australian accents and real workplaces before launch?
