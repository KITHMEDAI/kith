## Twitter/X

A lot of "is this video platform private enough" worry isn't really about the platform - it's not knowing when AI is active on a call, especially after an update quietly flips a default back on. Kith's notetaker bot only ever processes audio when you explicitly invite it into that specific session. Nothing ambient, nothing reset by an update.
