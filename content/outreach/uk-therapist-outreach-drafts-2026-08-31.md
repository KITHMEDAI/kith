## 1. Sol Matossian (Sol Therapy, London, solo)

**To:** sol@soltherapy.co.uk
**Subject:** A free note-writing tool for integrative practice

Hi Sol,

My name is Anurag, and I'm building Kith — a free clinical documentation tool for therapists, built around UK GDPR from the ground up rather than a US HIPAA product adapted after the fact.

For an integrative practice like yours, the part that might actually help: it listens through your device mic in person, or a notetaker bot on Google Meet for online sessions, and drafts a structured note afterward — you review and edit, not sign off blind. Recording consent and AI-processing consent are two separate opt-ins, not one bundled checkbox.

It's free, no card required, genuinely — not a trial funnel. If you'd ever want to try it on a real session and tell me honestly what's wrong with it, I'd welcome that — and no worries at all if it's not useful right now.
