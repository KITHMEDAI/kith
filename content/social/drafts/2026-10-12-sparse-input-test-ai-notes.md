## Twitter/X

Quick test for any AI note tool: give it deliberately thin notes.

If the output comes back with an affect description, a client quote, or an anxiety rating you never wrote, that's a red flag.

A thin section should stay thin. Try it on ours: kith.space/soap-formatter
