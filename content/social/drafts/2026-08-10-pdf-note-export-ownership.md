## Twitter/X

Session notes are supposed to outlive whatever software you wrote them in. If your practice tool doesn't let you export clean PDFs on your own terms, you don't really own your notes - you're renting access to them. Kith exports every note as a PDF, no support ticket required.

## Instagram

A session note is supposed to outlive the software it was written in. An insurance audit, a client transferring to another provider, or just switching practice-management tools a few years from now - all of that requires the note to leave the system it was written in cleanly, on your own timeline.

A lot of tools make that harder than it sounds. Exports gated behind a support request, formatting that breaks on the way out, fields that don't come through at all. If getting your own documentation out requires asking someone else's permission, you don't fully control it.

In Kith, every session note exports to PDF directly, whenever you need it - no request, no waiting on support. Free to start if you want to see how it holds up against whatever you're using now.
