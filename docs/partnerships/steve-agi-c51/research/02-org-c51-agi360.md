# Due diligence: C51 Consulting / AGI360 (Steve Agi)

Research date: 2026-10-18. Public sources only. Nothing was logged into, signed up for, submitted or contacted.
Labels: **CONFIRMED** means a primary source was seen (URL given). **LIKELY** means a strong inference from several signals. **UNVERIFIED** means it is claimed but nothing independent backs it.

---

## 1. Summary verdict

**This is a real, small, Melbourne-based operator. It is not a scam shell. But it is a very early-stage, essentially one-person brand ecosystem with no independently verifiable clients or commercial outcomes.** The most important finding is about the deal itself:

> **C51/KAOS AI already publishes its own "NDIS Documentation Assist" product**, a working demonstrator for NDIS progress notes, incident drafts, group notes and similar documents. It is listed on AGI360 as an "Agi360 Venture" where "Agi360 has a direct commercial interest", and it is actively seeking a paid pilot provider. (CONFIRMED: https://agi360.online/#/ventures/ndis-documentation-assist and https://ndis-ai-assistant-co-lsgj.bolt.host/)

So the proposed "Kith builds tech, Steve brings market" partnership is in a category where Steve already has a competing or prototype product, his own brand on it, and the customer-facing pilot channel. Kith could end up supplying the real engineering behind a C51/KAOS-branded product while C51 owns the customer relationships. This does not prove bad faith. It may simply be that Steve has validated demand and wants a real engineering partner. But it has to be put on the table and settled in writing before any work starts.

Overall: **proceed only with caution, and only under a written agreement.** Do no unpaid build work. Keep Kith's IP and branding. Make the existing NDIS demonstrator's status and ownership explicit.

## 3. What they sell and the business model

AGI360 calls itself "commercialisation infrastructure". In practice it is a consulting service ladder plus a listings site for opportunities and ventures. **Pricing is published openly** (CONFIRMED: https://agi360.online/#/ and https://agi360.online/#/market-access):

| Tier | Price (AUD, + GST) |
|---|---|
| Commercial Readiness Assessment ("front door") | from A$1,950 (A$950 appears in some places) |
| Commercialisation Sprint | from A$15,000 |
| **Market Access Program** | **A$950 onboarding (one-off) + A$495 per industry per month, minimum 2 industries (A$990/mo), maximum 5 (A$2,475/mo), recommended 3-month initial term**, plus affiliate or success commission "as agreed under partner terms" |
| Pilot Design & Market Validation | from A$25,000 |
| Commercial Pathways | custom |
| Commercial Execution Support | from A$4,950/month |
| Success-based arrangements | "Milestone fees, success fees, licensing participation, revenue share, introducer fees, **equity**, hybrid", for selected opportunities only |

**Market Access in detail:** it is aimed at businesses that *already* have an affiliate, referral or reseller programme, working tracking, pricing and the ability to fulfil sales. AGI360 is added to the client's referral system as an affiliate. It does sector positioning, outreach (LinkedIn, email, "industry contacts"), "selected introductions", lead tracking and monthly reports. The listed exclusions are explicit: no guaranteed leads, sales or meetings, no paid ads, no outsourced sales. **"Paying the onboarding fee does not automatically guarantee acceptance into ongoing Market Access."** "AGI360 retains discretion over who is approached, which introductions are made…" The client is responsible for "fulfilment and sales" and "commission payments".

So the economics are: the client pays fixed fees whatever the outcome, and pays commission on top if anything converts. The client carries all the outcome risk. This is a normal model for an agency, but it is the opposite of "shared upside".

**Other revenue lines** (CONFIRMED in site content): Partner/Sponsored Content (paid editorial), "Intelligence Partnerships" (commissioned research), events, and an affiliate link to GoHighLevel CRM carrying Steve's personal referral code (`fp_ref=steve-agi-c2975a`). The C51 site also sells small-business services: SLAM Local+ social and Google management from A$499/month, websites, "Business Pulse" pilots, and a 90-minute leakage review, diagnostic and 30-day proof-of-value.

**Brand stack** (CONFIRMED: https://c51consulting.com/about-us/): "C51 advises. KAOS delivers. CONTROL OS demonstrates. AI is OK teaches." AGI360 sits on top as the commercialisation and listings layer. That is five or more brands for what appears to be a one- or two-person firm.

## 6. Red flags

1. **Direct product overlap and a conflict of interest (high severity).** C51/KAOS already has an NDIS documentation product that AGI360 says it has a "direct commercial interest" in. Kith's engineering could be used to turn that demo into a real product under C51 branding.
2. **No verifiable clients, testimonials or outcomes (high).** Every case study is anonymous, and most "ventures" are Steve's own demonstrators.
3. **Very new public-facing assets (medium).** agi360.online is 7 weeks old, kaos-ai.services is 6 months old, and the C51 site was almost entirely rebuilt Aug–Oct 2026. This is a rapid pivot from brand consulting into "AI + commercialisation".
4. **AI-built, boilerplate-heavy sites (medium).** Built with bolt.new, stock imagery, heavy repeated frameworks ("Assess → Identify → Define…", "KAOS 6"), and a dense disclaimer layer. Not deceptive in itself, but the polish exceeds the substance.
5. **Breadth implausible for team size (medium).** Councils, NDIS, food labs, Bitcoin-mining farm heat, coffee loyalty, proptech, microgrids, and irrigation IP, with no named team. "Team details to be disclosed during qualification."
6. **Pay-to-play structure (medium).** Market Access charges fees up front, with no guarantees and full discretion kept by AGI360. A "shared-upside" pitch from someone whose standard offer is fee-first deserves scrutiny. Check whether the "partnership" quietly turns into a Sprint, Pilot Design or Execution Support invoice.
7. **Minor inconsistencies (low).** The www.agi360.io domain is printed but not registered. A Gmail address is the main contact. AGI360's Terms name no entity, ABN or governing law. The trading names appear not to be registered business names. The "Founded 2011" claim sits alongside a Pty Ltd incorporated in 2021. Listing dates precede the domain's creation.
8. **Unsubstantiated third-party claims (low–medium).** "Engineering validated", "Patent granted", "25–30% water savings", "Active pilot regions" are all given without sources, despite the site's own rule that "we do not publish unsupported outcome claims".
9. **Capital-raising language** (Crypto Crops "Seeking investment"; "investor mandate matching"). The site says it uses AFSL/CSF partners but names none. This is not relevant to Kith directly, but it shows the range of what is being attempted.

**How the classic "commercialisation partner" exploitation patterns compare:**

| Pattern | Present here? |
|---|---|
| Founder builds for free while the partner "brings the market" | **Risk present.** The proposal as described has Kith doing the tech, delivery and security, with Steve contributing non-measurable inputs (knowledge, positioning, partners) |
| Partner keeps the customer relationships | **Risk present.** Market Access terms say the client owns fulfilment but AGI360 controls outreach and introductions. NDIS pilot enquiries already route to C51's Gmail |
| Partner keeps or co-owns the IP / brand | **Risk present.** There is an existing C51/KAOS-branded NDIS product, and AGI360 terms claim ownership of "platform functionality" |
| Vague obligations ("introductions, not guaranteed") | **Present** in the standard Market Access terms |
| Upfront fees reframed as partnership | Possible. Watch for an "assessment" or "sprint" fee request |
| Equity grab | Possible. "Equity" is a listed success-based option |
| Outright fraud (fake entity, payment diversion) | **Not indicated.** The entity is real, GST-registered, and has consistent contacts and a long local identity |

## 7. Green flags

- A real Australian Pty Ltd (KAPAGI CONSULTING PTY LTD, ABN 59 647 485 783), active and GST-registered since 2021, in Melbourne (CONFIRMED).
- The same phone number and owner disclosure appear across sites. Steve Agi is a named WordPress author. His Melbourne publishing history goes back to 2009 (LIKELY same person).
- **Pricing is transparent and published.** That is unusual, and the opposite of a hidden-fee scheme.
- The disclaimers are unusually candid: no guaranteed outcomes, and listings openly state what is unproven, including on its own NDIS product.
- The existing NDIS demo shows real interest in the Australian NDIS documentation workflow and a sensible pilot design (baseline, success measures, human review, synthetic data first, no participant records by email).
- No scam reports or complaints were found anywhere.

## 8. Protective terms we should insist on

1. **Paid discovery or nothing.** No unpaid build beyond what Kith already has. Any custom NDIS work is either paid by Steve or C51, or paid by a named pilot customer.
2. **IP stays with Kith.** All Kith code, models, prompts, schemas and derivatives stay Kith's. Steve gets a non-exclusive, revocable reseller or referral right, not a licence to the source. No "joint IP".
3. **Settle the existing NDIS Documentation Assist product in writing.** Either it is retired or replaced by Kith (Kith branding, or "powered by Kith" at minimum), or it is a competing product and the deal is off. No running Kith output behind a C51/KAOS front end without a written white-label licence and fees.
4. **Kith holds the customer contracts and data.** Kith is the contracting party and data controller/processor of record for any provider. The Data Processing Agreement and privacy terms are Kith's. Patient or participant data never goes through C51 email or systems.
5. **Defined, measurable obligations for Steve.** For example: X qualified introductions with named providers within 60–90 days, each logged. If not met, exclusivity and any revenue share lapse automatically.
6. **Revenue share only on cash Kith actually collects** from customers Steve introduced and documented, with a sunset (for example 12–24 months per customer), no equity, and no fees on Kith's organic or inbound customers.
7. **No exclusivity**, or a narrow, time-boxed exclusivity (for example Community Participation providers in VIC, 6 months) tied to targets.
8. **No upfront fees to AGI360**, meaning no Readiness Assessment, Sprint or Market Access onboarding as a condition of "partnership".
9. **Non-solicit and non-compete in the NDIS documentation category** for the term plus 12 months, covering C51, KAOS AI, AGI360 and affiliates.
10. **Contracting entity is KAPAGI CONSULTING PTY LTD (ACN 647 485 783)**, not a trading name. Choose a governing law Kith can enforce, or arbitration. Termination for convenience on 30 days' notice.
11. **Branding and claims approval.** Steve may not make claims about Kith (security, compliance, outcomes) without written approval.

## 9. Open questions to ask Steve

1. Who built NDIS Documentation Assist, who owns it, and what happens to it if we partner? Is the plan to replace it with Kith?
2. Who else is in C51/KAOS (is there a "Chris B")? Who are the directors and shareholders of KAPAGI Consulting Pty Ltd?
3. Name two or three past clients (any sector) we can speak to, ideally in health, NDIS or aged care.
4. Which specific NDIS or allied health providers are already in conversation? Has the "pilot site identified" in the case study been named?
5. Is the proposed deal different from AGI360's paid tiers? Will any fee ever be payable by Kith?
6. What exactly does "partners and route-to-market" consist of: named channel partners, an associations list, an existing CRM?
7. Who would hold the customer contract, the data processing role and support obligations?
8. Are "C51 Consulting" and "AGI360" registered business names, and why does the site reference agi360.io?
9. What NDIS, health-privacy or security credentials or advisors does C51 have access to?
10. What happened with the "subscription selling" that was removed from the NDIS demo? Did it ever take payments or sign-ups?
