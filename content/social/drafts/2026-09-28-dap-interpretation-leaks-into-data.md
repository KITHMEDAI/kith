## Twitter/X

The most common DAP note slip: interpretation leaking into Data.

"Client seems to be minimizing" isn't data. "Said the week was 'fine'; flat affect, looked away when work came up" is.

The read belongs in Assessment. Keep that line clean and the note holds up on a re-read.
