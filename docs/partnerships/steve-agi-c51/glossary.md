# Glossary: words Steve is likely to use

Plain-English explanations for the 20 Oct call and beyond. ⭐ = most likely to come up.

## 1. NDIS basics

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **NDIS** | National Disability Insurance Scheme. Australia's government scheme that funds support for people with permanent, significant disability. Started 2013; around 700k people use it. Huge market, heavily regulated. |
| **NDIA** | National Disability Insurance Agency. The government agency that **runs** the scheme: approves people and funds their plans. |
| ⭐ **NDIS Commission** | NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission. The **regulator**: registers providers, runs audits, receives incident reports and complaints, and can ban providers. Who providers are scared of. |
| ⭐ **Participant** | The person with a disability who receives NDIS funding. (Don't say "patient". In NDIS they're participants or clients.) |
| ⭐ **NDIS plan** | The participant's funded plan: their **goals** and the budget for supports to reach them. Reviewed roughly yearly. |
| **Plan review / reassessment** | When the NDIA re-checks a plan. Providers write **progress reports** as evidence. This decides future funding, so documentation quality really matters. |
| ⭐ **Provider** | An organisation or sole trader delivering supports (support work, therapy, accommodation…). Your potential customers. |
| ⭐ **Registered vs unregistered provider** | Registered providers passed Commission audits and must meet the Practice Standards. Unregistered ones have lighter rules but can't serve some participants. Registered providers have the heaviest documentation load, so they're the best target. |
| **Agency / plan / self-managed** | Three ways a participant's money is managed. "Agency-managed" participants can only use **registered** providers. |
| **Plan manager** | A company that handles a participant's invoices and payments. |
| **Support coordinator** | Helps a participant find and coordinate providers. Writes a lot of reports too. |
| **LAC** | Local Area Coordinator. Community partners that help participants with plans. |
| **Pricing Arrangements (formerly "Price Guide")** | The NDIA's official list of what providers can charge per hour, per support type. |
| **Core / Capacity Building / Capital** | The three funding categories: daily support / building skills (incl. therapy) / equipment and home mods. |

## 2. Types of NDIS service (where documentation happens)

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Support worker** | Frontline staff who help participants day to day (personal care, shopping, community access). Often casual, often on their phone, often writing notes at the end of a shift. **This is the "raw notes" user Steve described.** |
| ⭐ **SIL** | Supported Independent Living. 24/7 support for participants in shared houses. Lots of shifts, lots of notes, high audit risk. SIL providers had to register by **1 Oct 2026**, so many face their **first audits** now. |
| **SDA** | Specialist Disability Accommodation. The housing itself (building), not the support. |
| **STA / respite** | Short Term Accommodation: short stays, often giving family carers a break. |
| **Community access / participation** | Support to go out: social activities, appointments, etc. |
| ⭐ **Behaviour support** | Specialists who write **Behaviour Support Plans** for participants with behaviours of concern. |
| ⭐ **Restrictive practices** | Anything restricting a person's freedom: physical/mechanical/chemical restraint, seclusion, environmental restrictions (e.g. locked fridge). Heavily regulated and **must be reported monthly**. Very sensitive. |

## 3. Documentation words (the product's core)

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Progress notes / case notes / shift notes** | The record written after each shift or session: what happened, what support was given, how the participant went. Roughly the same thing; "case notes" is common in community services. |
| ⭐ **Goal-linked notes** | Notes that connect what happened to the participant's **NDIS plan goals** ("practised catching the bus, which supports goal 2: independent travel"). Auditors want this; "helped with shopping" alone is a fail. **Kith's biggest value-add.** |
| **Task-based notes** | The bad kind: a list of tasks with no outcomes or goals. |
| ⭐ **Incident report** | A written report when something goes wrong (injury, abuse, unauthorised restraint, death…). **Reportable incidents** must go to the Commission, mostly **within 24 hours**, plus a detailed follow-up within 5 business days. |
| **Progress report** | Longer report (often by therapists) summarising progress toward goals over months. Used in plan reviews. Takes 2–4 hours to write. |
| **Person-centred** | Writing and supporting from the participant's perspective and choices. Expected language in notes. |
| **Choice and control** | Core NDIS principle: the participant decides. Notes should reflect it. |
| **Objective vs subjective** | Notes must state observed facts ("she said she was tired, slept 2 hrs") over opinions ("she was lazy"). An AI that rewrites judgemental phrasing into objective language is valuable. |
| ⭐ **Human-in-the-loop / mandatory human review** | The AI drafts; a human checks and approves. Steve explicitly asked for this. Kith already works this way. |
| **Service agreement** | Contract between a provider and participant about what supports will be delivered. |
| **Service bookings / claims** | Billing the NDIA for delivered hours. Notes are the evidence that the support actually happened, so bad notes create billing risk. |

## 4. Audits and compliance (NDIS-specific)

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **NDIS Practice Standards** | The rulebook registered providers are audited against (incl. documentation, incident management, risk, rights). |
| **Quality Indicators** | Specific things auditors check for each standard. |
| ⭐ **Audit: verification vs certification** | Two audit levels. Lower-risk providers get a lighter *verification* audit; higher-risk ones (e.g. SIL, behaviour support) get a full *certification* audit. Notes are a key part of what auditors check. |
| **Approved Quality Auditor** | Third-party firms that run the audits. |
| **Worker Screening Check** | Background check every NDIS worker needs. (Not our problem, but you'll hear it.) |
| **Non-conformity** | An audit failure finding. Providers dread these. |

## 5. Allied health and other health terms

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Allied health** | Health professionals who aren't doctors or nurses: psychologists, OTs, speech pathologists, physios, dietitians. **Kith's current home turf.** |
| **OT** | Occupational therapist. Huge in NDIS (function, daily living, home mods). |
| **Speechie** | Aussie slang for speech pathologist. |
| ⭐ **AHPRA** (Ahpra) | Regulator that registers health practitioners (psychologists, OTs, physios…). Has published **guidance on AI scribes**: the practitioner stays responsible, must get patient consent and must check the notes. |
| **Medicare / MBS / Better Access** | Australia's public health insurance. Better Access = Medicare-funded psychology sessions (GP mental health plan). Different funding from NDIS. |
| **GP** | General practitioner (family doctor). |
| **Aged care / Support at Home** | Elderly care sector. New Aged Care Act and the "Support at Home" program started late 2025. Similar documentation pain to NDIS; possible future market. |
| ⭐ **AI scribe** | The Aussie term for AI that listens to consults and writes notes (Heidi, Lyrebird…). Kith is an AI scribe for therapy. |

## 6. Privacy, security and regulation (Australia)

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Privacy Act 1988** | Australia's main privacy law. Health info is "sensitive information" with stricter rules. |
| ⭐ **APPs** | Australian Privacy Principles: the 13 rules under the Privacy Act. |
| ⭐ **APP 8 / cross-border disclosure** | Sending personal info overseas (e.g. to US AI servers, or to staff in India). Allowed, but the provider stays **accountable** for what happens overseas. This is why buyers want **AU hosting**. |
| ⭐ **Data sovereignty / data residency** | "Is the data stored in Australia?" Expect this question. |
| **OAIC** | Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, the privacy regulator. |
| ⭐ **NDB scheme** | Notifiable Data Breaches. Serious breaches must be reported to OAIC and affected people. Health is the #1 breached sector. |
| **Health Records Acts** | Extra state privacy laws (e.g. VIC Health Records Act 2001, NSW HRIP Act). Melbourne = Victoria, so the VIC Act applies. |
| **Consent to record** | Most states require consent from everyone before recording a conversation. Matters for voice features. |
| **PIA** | Privacy Impact Assessment: a document analysing privacy risks of a new system. Good to offer for a pilot. |
| ⭐ **Essential Eight** | The Australian government's (ASD/ACSC) 8 baseline cyber-security controls (patching, MFA, backups…). Common buyer checkbox. |
| **ISO 27001 / SOC 2** | International security certifications. Big buyers ask; small providers often don't for a pilot. |
| **IRAP** | Australian government security assessment. Only if selling to government. Ignore for now. |
| ⭐ **TGA** | Therapeutic Goods Administration (like the FDA). Software that **diagnoses or suggests treatment** can count as a medical device. Kith must stay **documentation only**. |
| **SaMD** | Software as a Medical Device, the TGA category to avoid. |
| **DPA** | Data Processing Agreement: contract on how a vendor handles customer data. Buyers will want one from us. |

## 7. Business / commercialisation words (Steve's language)

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Commercialisation** | Turning a product or idea into something that earns money: finding customers, pricing, selling. Steve's whole pitch. |
| ⭐ **Route to market / go-to-market (GTM)** | The plan for how you reach and sell to customers (direct sales, partners, resellers…). |
| ⭐ **Product-market fit (PMF)** | When the product solves a problem people will actually pay for. Kith doesn't have it yet; that's the honest gap. |
| ⭐ **Discovery** | Talking to potential customers to learn their real problems *before* building. "Structured discovery" = organised interviews. |
| ⭐ **Pilot** | A small, time-limited trial with a real customer to prove value. |
| **Pilot validation** | Proving the pilot worked (agreed metrics, e.g. time saved per note, audit-ready notes). |
| **Qualified opportunity / qualified lead** | A prospect confirmed to have the problem, budget and authority to buy. Not just "interested". |
| **Pipeline** | All the potential deals in progress. "Build the pipeline" = find and nurture leads. |
| **Market development / market access** | Steve's service: finding and opening doors to a market. |
| **Value proposition** | One-line reason a customer should care. |
| **Positioning** | How the product is described relative to competitors. |
| ⭐ **Shared upside / revenue share** | Both sides earn a % of the revenue instead of one paying the other upfront. |
| **Equity** | Ownership shares in a company. (Steve's site mentions equity deals; be careful.) |
| ⭐ **IP (intellectual property)** | Ownership of the code, designs, brand, know-how. "Who owns the IP" = who owns what gets built. |
| ⭐ **Market rights / exclusivity** | Right to sell in a territory (e.g. Australia). *Exclusive* = only that partner can. |
| **Licence / licensing** | Letting someone use your IP (e.g. your engine) without owning it. |
| **White-label** | Your product sold under someone else's brand. ("Powered by Kith" is the middle ground.) |
| **Reseller / channel partner** | Someone who sells your product to their customers for a cut. |
| **MOU** | Memorandum of Understanding: a non-binding written agreement on intentions. Common first paper. |
| **Term sheet / heads of agreement** | Short document listing the key deal terms before a full contract. ("Heads of agreement" is the Aussie phrase.) |
| **NDA** | Non-Disclosure Agreement: both sides agree to keep shared info confidential. |
| **Scope / scoped arrangement** | Clearly defined what's included and what isn't. |
| **Milestones** | Defined checkpoints (e.g. pilot live, 10 users) often tied to payments or rights. |
| **Pty Ltd / ABN / ACN** | Australian private company / business number / company number. (Steve's: KAPAGI Consulting Pty Ltd.) |
| **GST** | Australia's 10% goods and services tax. |
