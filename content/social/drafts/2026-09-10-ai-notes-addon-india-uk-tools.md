## Twitter/X

Went through five practice-management tools for India and five for the UK. AI session notes are almost always a pricier separate tier - WriteUpp's AI Scribe plan costs ~2x its base Solo plan, and India's ProTherapy doesn't offer it at all. Kith bundles it into the free plan.
