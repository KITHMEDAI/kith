## Twitter/X

A patient agreeing to be recorded and a patient agreeing to have that recording run through AI are two different consents. Most intake forms still treat them as one checkbox - which is exactly the gap India's DPDP Act calls out.

## Instagram

"Is it okay if I record this session?" and "is it okay if AI processes this recording?" sound like the same question. Under India's DPDP Act, they're not - consent has to be specific to a purpose, and recording a session is a different purpose from running that recording through an AI note-taker.

Most intake paperwork still bundles both into one checkbox, signed once and forgotten. It's a small gap, but it's the kind that shows up the moment a patient asks exactly what they agreed to.

Kith keeps these as two separate consent gates instead of one - recording and AI processing are tracked and granted independently. It doesn't make the underlying question less worth asking your patients directly. It just means the record of their answer holds up.
