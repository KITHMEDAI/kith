## Twitter/X

A SOAP template doesn't remove the hard part of writing a note - that was never the formatting. It's knowing what actually belongs in each section, and how much to say. Put together a free, copyable template with a worked example: kith.space/blog/soap-note-templates

## Instagram

A blank SOAP template still has a blank problem underneath it. Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan gives you four labeled boxes - it doesn't tell you what belongs in each one, how much detail is too much, or where a stray observation actually goes.

That's the part that actually eats time, not the acronym. So instead of just another template, we wrote one out with a full worked example next to it, plus the mistakes that are easy to make even once you know the structure (vague Plans, Assessment that's really just Subjective repeated back, that kind of thing).

Free, no signup: kith.space/blog/soap-note-templates
