# Promotion Channel Ideas (research only — no actions taken)

Compiled 2026-08-16. This is a reviewed list for a human to prioritize, same
spirit as `docs/outreach-research-runbook.md`: research produces candidates,
a human decides what to actually do. Nothing here has been signed up for,
posted to, or contacted. Existing channels already in active use (X/Twitter,
Reddit via `docs/daily-outreach-runbook.md`, LinkedIn 1:1 outreach) aren't
repeated below except where there's a gap worth flagging.

  today's Steve Agi outreach — a DM sent from that account never landed,

  Every other LinkedIn idea below is weaker until this is diagnosed — worth

## Low-effort, low-risk (self-serve, no cold contact)

- **Software directories: G2, Capterra, GetApp.** Real, active category —
  Capterra alone has a "Mental Health EHR Software" and "Psychiatry EMR"
  category with TherapyNotes, Sessions Health, etc. already listed (confirmed
  live via search today). Creating a vendor profile is free, self-serve, and
  it's exactly where a therapist doing comparison shopping (the audience
  `kith-vs-simplepractice`/`kith-vs-upheal`/`kith-vs-mentalyc` blog posts are
  already written for) ends up. Zero consent/outreach risk — it's a listing,
  not contact.
- **Product Hunt launch.** One-time high-visibility event, entirely
  self-initiated (no cold contact). Needs prep: a hunter (ideally someone
  with an existing PH following), launch-day assets, and a clear "why now."
  Worth timing to a real feature ship, not just a generic re-launch.
- **YouTube: short demo/explainer videos.** A 2-3 minute "here's ambient
  transcription end to end" video embeds well in blog posts and social
  drafts, and gives something more concrete than a screenshot for the
  Facebook-group and directory audiences below. Reuse the pitch one-pager's
  structure as a script outline.

## Medium-effort, needs genuine participation (same rules as Reddit)

- **Therapist Facebook groups** — real, active, large communities exist:
  Therapists in Private Practice (TIPP), Private Practice Startup, All
  Things Private Practice: Startup/Networking/Community/Support, Therapist
  Toolbox. Same hard rule as r/therapists in `docs/daily-outreach-runbook.md`:
  genuine participation first, tool mention only when it fits and isn't the
  point of the comment — these groups are just as quick to call out an ad as
  Reddit is, often faster since admins moderate directly.
- **Podcasts aimed at private-practice therapists** (practice-building /
  clinical-business shows, not clinical-technique shows). Guest-appearance
  angle: talk about documentation burden and AI consent design, not a sales
  pitch — same "genuine value first" principle as everything else in this
  file.
- **Guest posts / backlinks on adjacent blogs** — practice-building coaches,
  therapist SEO/marketing consultants, compliance-focused blogs (DPDP/GDPR
  angle Kith's own blog already covers). Two-way value: real backlink for
  Kith's SEO, real content for their audience.
- **LinkedIn creator/newsletter outreach**, same shape as today's Steve Agi
  message — AI-adjacent or healthtech newsletter writers, one at a time,
  genuinely tailored per person, not a template blast.

## Needs a real decision before touching (associations, sponsorship)

- **Professional associations** — BACP (UK), and India's equivalent
  clinical-psychology/counselling bodies. Most restrict direct vendor
  self-promotion to members but often have sponsor/partner listing tiers or
  accept submitted CE-eligible webinars. Worth a human decision on whether
  a paid sponsorship or a genuinely useful free webinar (e.g. "consent
  design for AI notetaking") is the right entry point — not something to
  pursue speculatively without knowing the actual terms.
