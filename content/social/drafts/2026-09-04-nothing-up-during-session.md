## Twitter/X

The usual pitch for AI note-taking is "save time after the session." The bigger thing it can fix is what's happening during it - a laptop or tablet open in front of a client reads as a barrier no matter how good the app is. Kith's in-person capture runs off the device mic. Nothing open, nothing to look at, nothing to type into mid-session.
