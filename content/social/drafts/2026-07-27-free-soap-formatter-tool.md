## Twitter/X

Built a free tool that takes whatever you scribbled during a session and turns it into a proper SOAP note (or EMDR: target/SUD/VOC/phase). No signup, no catch. kith.space/soap-formatter

## Instagram

Most of the time the hard part of documentation isn't knowing the format - it's turning three lines of shorthand from mid-session into something that actually reads like a note.

We built a small free tool for that: paste your rough notes or a transcript excerpt, and it structures them into a proper SOAP note - or, if that's the framework you're working in, into target/SUD/VOC/phase for EMDR.

No account, no signup, nothing saved. It's the same formatting engine that runs inside Kith, just available on its own for anyone who wants to try it.
