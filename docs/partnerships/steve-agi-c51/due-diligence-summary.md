## Verdict

**A real person with a real company. Probably not a scam, but high risk on delivery
and IP.** The main danger isn't losing money; it's Kith doing unpaid engineering that
ends up inside Steve's own NDIS product, with "shared upside" that never turns into
revenue. Proceed, but only with written terms and a pilot that pays or has milestone
payments.

## Market reality (from research/03)

- Generic AI notes are already crowded in AU (ShiftCare A$9–25/user/mo with AI notes;
  Splose, CordoCare, KoalaOne; Heidi and Lyrebird scribes). Brevity, SupportAbility
  and CareMaster customers have no AI → room for a bolt-on tool that works alongside them.
- Best wedge for support providers: raw notes/voice → **goal-linked** case notes, plus
  an audit-readiness check, with mandatory human review.
- **Compliance gap for Kith:** AI and transcription calls to US endpoints count as
  overseas disclosure under APP 8. We'd need AU-region hosting (Supabase/Vercel Sydney)
  and AU AI endpoints (e.g. Claude on Bedrock with AU routing). India-based support
  access must also be disclosed.
- No diagnosis or treatment suggestions (the TGA is enforcing against scribes that do this).
- Typical pilot: 8–12 weeks, 5–15 users, paid at a discount (A$2–5k or A$30–60/user/mo),
  with success metrics and conversion price agreed upfront.

## Protections to insist on (scam-proofing)

1. Contract with the **named legal entity** (KAPAGI Pty Ltd), signed by an actual director.
2. **Kith keeps its IP and engine.** Steve gets a licence or revenue share, not ownership.
3. **Put his NDIS Documentation Assist on the table:** retire it, merge it under clear
   terms, or agree in writing it won't compete.
4. **No unpaid build beyond a small demo.** Pilot customers pay (even discounted), or
   payments are tied to milestones.
5. **Kith holds the customer contracts and the data**, or at minimum is a direct party
   to them. Never be invisible behind his brand.
6. **No fees to AGI360.** Revenue share applies only to cash actually collected.
7. **Any exclusivity is earned:** e.g. named intros by date X, signed pilot by date Y.
8. Short initial term (e.g. 3–6 months) with a clean exit clause.
