## Twitter/X

Kith's capture is fully automatic within its scope, and that's also its edge: mic for in-person, a notetaker bot for Google Meet online. No upload option, no dictate-a-recap fallback. If a session wasn't captured live through one of those two paths, there's no note to generate.
